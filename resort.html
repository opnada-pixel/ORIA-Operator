The ritual

Five steps, in order.

Warm mineral water, held heat, a break of cold. Stillness holds the effect. Then it begins again — two or three times across a day.

  1. 01Immersionthe body is prepared, not treated
  2. 02Heatdry and vapour, held long enough to matter
  3. 03The breakcold, brief, deliberate
  4. 04Stillnessheated stone, nothing asked of you
  5. 05Againthe sequence, not the room

Measured, not asserted.

ORIA proposes to open as the world's largest thermal bathing facility — a Guinness World Records™ attempt, defined with the record body before design freeze.

Why it belongs to the concept

ORIA builds the thermal world first. The record would measure exactly that — so the headline and the product are the same thing.

The proposal

One facility, one figure, one new category.

01What is proposed

A title for the thermal facility at ORIA — applied for, adjudicated and verified in the normal way, before the resort opens.

02What is measured

The built area given over to thermal water, heat and recovery: 30,000 m² at design target.

03Why a new category

The existing record measures a whole resort's site. ORIA would measure only the thermal world inside it.

The two figures

Why the smaller number is the point.

Drawn to true relative area, one sits inside the other. One is a resort; the other is the bathing facility inside one — a city compared with a building in it.

The water

The water is either found or it is made — and neither answer is claimed early. Either way, the sequence, the temperatures and the rooms are identical.

Two scenarios

Two answers. One design.

The project's first technical question, carried in the design from the start.

Scenario A

The water is found

A natural thermal resource, proven by hydrogeological survey and test bore, then an abstraction licence and a sustainable-yield model — before a word is said about it in public.

Scenario B

The water is made

Heated and mineralised in a closed loop. No abstraction licence is required, and the public language says so plainly: engineered thermal water.

Survey not yet commissioned. No natural thermal or mineral claim will be made until it supports one.

Water discipline

Four measures, in both scenarios.

01Closed-loop filtration

Recirculation designed into the plant, not added to it.

02Continuous monitoring

Quality measured continuously, recorded and auditable.

03Pathogen control

Designed into the plant rather than managed by procedure.

04Evaporation replacement

Quantified, and reported in the published balance.
